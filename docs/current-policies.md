# Current Policy Index

Use this index when repository design records disagree. The [README](../README.md) and [first-installation guide](installation.md) describe current operation. Files under `docs/superpowers/specs` and `docs/superpowers/plans` record how the application evolved; a document labelled Historical or Superseded is retained as design history and is not current policy.

## Current operating policy

- Scope is college scheduling for Regular, OJT, Tour and First-Year/OVPSA students. K-12, `SPECIALIZED` and the staged generate/publish workflow are retired.
- Staff and students use separate sessions. Staff onboarding requires email verification and temporary-password replacement. An authenticated active student may read Schedule, history, Notifications and Results, mark their own notices read, and download their own official Laboratory files and issued PE certificates without email registration or verification. Student login requires a nonblank complete middle name and matches it case-insensitively.
- Email verification is required before entering a Laboratory upload/edit workspace, initializing or loading a draft, and every add/remove/edit/cancel/finalize/submit-changes operation. The server checks the current database identity before processing an upload body. Verification never grants clinical eligibility or cross-student access; pending replacement email retains existing verified capability.
- Results overview reads create no draft or storage changes. Current and historical official files use configured Manila academic-year closing dates; an edit draft does not hide a finalized official revision. Draft, superseded, invalidated, deleted and pending-deletion files remain inaccessible, and revoked certificates remain nondownloadable.
- Verification continuation accepts only `/student`, `/student/results`, and `/student/results/<UUID>`; missing/rejected values fall back to `/student`. Confirmation is explicit and creates no session. Original-tab polling returns to the validated destination, stops on session expiry, and never replays uploads or mutations. A stale workspace requiring verification clears selected files.
- SMTP remains required for installation/staff onboarding and student Laboratory verification. A delivery outage may delay first upload verification but does not block authenticated portal reading/downloads. Portal notices remain readable independently of email; verified-address notification eligibility, current-state catch-up, deduplication and encrypted bounded outbox retry remain required.
- Scheduling uses Manila dates and weekdays. Laboratory precedes Physical Examination. First-Year/OVPSA keeps its owned reservations, external Laboratory accounting and minimum seven-calendar-day PE gap.
- Configured service capacity is the ceiling. Fresh installations seed independent daily maximums of 100 for KABALAKA Laboratory and CPU Physical Examination after migrations. Administrators can save any positive integer, including values above 100; migration/default changes and seed replay preserve existing saved rows. Automatic and manual replacements remain in the original configured academic cycle; exhaustion enters Manual Resolution.
- The clinic calendar is unified. Closure changes use impact review and explicit recovery; reopening a date only restores availability.
- Laboratory clinical completion follows the versioned CBC/Urine/Stool/applicable X-ray checklist. Laboratory document uploads remain separate and support revision/edit/resubmit. Physical Examination student uploads are retired; CPU Clinic or an Administrator records the physician finding and issues a private immutable JPG certificate in the completion transaction.
- Completion ownership comes from immutable checklist year/category and valid First-Year/OVPSA provenance. Fourth-year OJT requires manual CBC/Urine/Stool before PE; issuance confirms hospital X-ray and completes its four-test Laboratory checklist. Valid First-Year/OVPSA starts ready at 0/4 and confirms all four hospital tests during PE, clearing **Awaiting confirmation at Physical Examination**. Other categories retain full manual Laboratory completion. One contextual attestation is required. Preview/cancellation write no confirmation; final Laboratory, PE, certificate, audit and notification writes are atomic. Certificate correction/revocation preserves Laboratory confirmation. First-Year creates an external result without a draft/file; OJT document upload status stays separate.
- Cancelling a First-Year batch releases its scheduling membership/reservations while retaining completed visits. Their certificate correction uses immutable historical ownership and saved Laboratory confirmation/result evidence; new issuance still requires the active current published batch.
- Bulk replacement reviews 1–100 eligible appointments in one service, clinic, and academic year against a chosen date, then saves the whole selection atomically. A partially verified Laboratory visit retains its checklist through an explicit replacement and is protected from automatic displacement.
- Current schedules exclude academic years after their stored Manila closing date. Staff and students retain authorized historical records and downloads. The clinic calendar reports internal capacity by service and published college groups without exposing clinical content.
- Persisted generic imports require category/year and service-date provenance; batches are `PUBLISHED`/`CANCELLED`, items `SCHEDULED`. `NEEDS_REVIEW` flags inconsistent children. OVPSA revision/batch construction, appointment capacity holds and Laboratory editing retain their live `DRAFT`/`VALIDATED` states. Maximum capacity alone is stored and must be positive.
- Effective appointment lineage, import-backed immutable snapshots, result revisions, private file authorization, audit events and encrypted retryable outbox delivery remain required.
